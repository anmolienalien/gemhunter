Notes

  • Covers PSA 10s that averaged $100–$3,000 over the last three months, so you can widen the price range up to those limits.
  • "Fair value" blends the card's own recent sales with an estimate from similar cards (PSA 10 pop, gem rate, Pokémon popularity, card type, set, era, language and edition). The more sales a card has, the more its own price counts.
  • −30% means it trades 30% below that estimate; +30% means 30% above.
  • Hover a value to see the closest comparison cards and how much weight the card's own sales got.
  • It's a screening aid, not a price guarantee or financial advice. Things the model can't see, like condition trends, artwork appeal or upcoming reprints, move prices too.
  • Prices come from PSA in US dollars; AUD is converted at the rate shown at the bottom of the page.
